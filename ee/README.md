Code in any folder named `ee`, here or anywhere else in the repository, is
licensed under the Enterprise License in [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md). It is
free to read and to try outside production. Production or business use needs
a paid subscription.

Everything outside `ee` folders is the free core, under the PolyForm Internal
Use or PolyForm Noncommercial license in the repository's
[`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md). Any company may use the core for free for its
internal operations, and anyone for noncommercial purposes. Selling it, or
hosting it for others, needs a commercial license from CommonIndex.

There are no enterprise features yet. When one is added, it goes in an `ee`
folder beside the code it extends, for example `commonindex/ee/` or `web/src/ee/`,
and the core works without it.
