# Code of Conduct

CommonIndex is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of
them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else
you represent the project.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the
maintainers. On GitHub, use **Report content** on the comment or issue, or
contact a maintainer directly. We will review every report, keep the reporter's
identity confidential, and respond as quickly as we can.

## What happens next

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban the people responsible, for a
time or permanently.
